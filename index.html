01 · The Event

What is NASA Space Apps Challenge?

NASA Space Apps Challenge is the world's largest annual global hackathon. Participants around the world use NASA and partner space agency data to solve real-world problems. Teams can work on topics including climate, Earth science, space exploration, technology, storytelling, and more.

02 · The City

Why Tripoli?

Tripoli is one of the oldest cities in the Mediterranean and one of the youngest in spirit. It holds a dense concentration of universities, students, and self-taught builders whose work rarely reaches a global stage. Hosting Space Apps here puts open NASA data directly in the hands of that talent, connects the city to a worldwide network of teams solving the same problems on the same weekend, and shows that world-class innovation does not need to leave North Lebanon to be taken seriously.
